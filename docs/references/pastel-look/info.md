## What it shows
An AI-generated-looking collage of UI cards ("SOOTH ART" etc. — the text is gibberish, so take only the style, not the content). Rounded panels float on a dusty-rose gradient background with soft 3D "clay" objects: spheres, discs, wave shapes, puffy blobs.

### Style notes
- **Shapes:** large corner radius (≈ 16–24 px on cards, full pills on inputs/buttons), no hard borders.
- **Depth:** very soft, wide, low-opacity drop shadows (e.g. `0 20px 40px rgba(120,90,90,0.15)`) plus subtle inner highlights → soft-UI / neumorphic-lite. 3D props are matte clay with gentle top-left lighting.
- **Composition:** overlapping cards, colour-blocked halves (peach | cream), flowing wave shapes in blue/cream cutting across cards, small floating spheres sprinkled between cards for depth.
- **Inputs/list rows:** white pill fields with a tiny coloured circle icon at one end; list items are white rounded rows with a pastel dot avatar and a small radio/indicator at the right.
- **Typography:** small, light/regular geometric sans (Inter/Poppins-like), dark grey; very little text; generous whitespace.
- **Mood:** calm, airy, low contrast, warm-leaning pastels with a cool powder-blue counterpoint.

### Takeaways for the app
- Base: cream surface on dusty-rose gradient; accents peach + powder blue + blush; one deeper blue (#6888a1) for emphasis/contrast-safe text on light.
- Soft large shadows, big radii, pill buttons; decorative blurred blobs/spheres in the background.
- Check contrast — the palette is low-contrast; keep body text ≥ #4a4a4a on cream.
