### Short description (~300 characters)

My Gift Lockers keeps everything you need to buy someone a gift: their clothing, shoe and ring sizes, birthdays, anniversaries, favorite things and wish lists. Invite people to fill in their own details, get reminder emails before every occasion, and run Secret Santa exchanges. Free.

### Long description

Buying gifts shouldn't mean texting someone "what size are you?" every December.

My Gift Lockers gives each person you shop for a locker that holds their clothing, shoe and ring sizes, birthday and anniversary, favorite colors and things they love, things to avoid, and their wish lists. Send someone an invite link and they fill it in themselves. They can keep it up to date and choose what you see.

- **Sizes that travel:** sizes are converted between US, UK, EU, Japan, Brazil, Mexico and Colombia, so a gift ordered abroad still fits.
- **Reminders:** emails before birthdays, anniversaries and holidays, so nothing sneaks up on you.
- **Gift ideas:** suggestions for each person based on everything in their locker.
- **Kids:** keep growing kids' sizes current.
- **Secret Santa:** invite everyone with one link, collect each person's top wishes, set exclusions and draw names. Only the organizer needs an account.

Free, in five languages, and it works on any phone or computer.

### Maker comment (Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, Show HN)

> Every holiday I ended up texting family "what size are you now?" and getting it wrong anyway, especially for relatives abroad, where sizes are labeled differently. So I built My Gift Lockers: one place for the sizes, dates and wish lists of everyone I buy for. It's free. I'd love feedback, especially on the invite flow and the size conversions.

## Where to submit, in order

The holidays are peak season for gift and Secret Santa searches, so the order is about speed: quick, high-value listings first, then the launch.

Add `?utm_source=<site>` to the URL you submit (e.g. `https://www.mygiftlockers.com/?utm_source=alternativeto`) to see which ones send visitors. The canonical tags keep these from counting as duplicate pages.

### Tier 1: this week

| Site | Why | What to submit |
|---|---|---|
| **AlternativeTo** (alternativeto.net) | People search "Elfster alternative" and land here. A strong, lasting link. | Add as an alternative to **Elfster, DrawNames, Giftster**. Short + long description, logo, category "Lifestyle". |
| **SaaSHub** (saashub.com) | Also alternatives-based; good link. | Same alternatives and descriptions. |
| **Product Hunt** (producthunt.com) | The biggest single launch-day spike. | Plan it, don't rush: 4–6 screenshots, maker comment, and a weekday launch (Tuesday–Thursday, 12:01 am Pacific). Early December fits the season. |

### Tier 2: next 1–2 weeks (a few minutes each)

| Site | Notes |
|---|---|
| **BetaList** | Free queue can take weeks; paid skip-the-line is optional, not needed. |
| **Uneed** | Free listing with a waiting line. |
| **Fazier** | Free launch. |
| **Indie Hackers** (product page) | Post the maker story; the community is friendly to solo builders. |
| **Launching Next**, **SideProjectors**, **Tiny Startups**, **Peerlist** | Quick free listings. |

Check each site's current pricing before submitting; several now push paid fast-track options. Skip any that ask for a reciprocal backlink.

### Tier 3: communities (post as yourself, follow each place's rules)

- **Reddit r/SideProject:** "I built a free app to keep family's sizes and wish lists in one place." Share the story, not an ad.
- **Hacker News (Show HN):** title `Show HN: My Gift Lockers – sizes, birthdays and wish lists for people you buy for`, with the maker comment as the first comment.
- Avoid posting into Secret Santa or parenting subreddits unless their rules allow self-promotion.

## Outreach: getting into existing articles

These articles rank for "best Secret Santa app/generator" and get refreshed every season. Ask to be added. Authors care about what's different, which here is that the locker lasts all year and works across countries.

- [PopSci: The best Secret Santa platforms, ranked](https://www.popsci.com/?p=173180)
- [Women Love Tech: The best Secret Santa apps](https://womenlovetech.com/the-best-secret-santa-apps-to-download-this-christmas/)
- [Streamer.guide: Best Secret Santa apps](https://streamer.guide/guides/best-secret-santa-apps)
- [The News Minute: 15 Secret Santa generators compared](https://www.thenewsminute.com/news/best-online-secret-santa-generator-compare-15-websites-choose-top-one-170498)

Skip competitor-run lists (GiftList, Uplup): they won't add a rival.

For the **size charts**, pitch gift-guide, expat and parenting bloggers: the charts cover Brazil, Mexico and Colombia, which most charts leave out.
