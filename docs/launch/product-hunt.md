## Product Hunt's rule on votes

Product Hunt forbids asking for upvotes or trading votes, and it detects bursts of votes from new
accounts. Ask people to **check it out and leave feedback**, never to "upvote". The templates below
follow that.

## Answers to likely questions

- **"How do you make money?"** It's free. Gift ideas can include affiliate links (Amazon, other
  stores), which is disclosed on the page; that's how it's funded.
- **"What about privacy?"** People share only what they choose, and they can see and change who
  sees what. Addresses and birthdays are never shown to Secret Santa matches. Full policy:
  mygiftlockers.com/privacy.
- **"Is there an app?"** It works in any phone or computer browser today. An iPhone app is in
  development.
- **"How are gift ideas made?"** An AI model suggests ideas from what's in the person's locker;
  you get a short list with reasons and store links.
- **"Why not Elfster / DrawNames?"** Those are great for the draw. My Gift Lockers keeps each
  person's sizes and wish lists all year, converts sizes across countries, and works in 5
  languages.
