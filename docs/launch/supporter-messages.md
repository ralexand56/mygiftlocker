# Supporter messages: Product Hunt launch (Tue, Dec 8, 2026)

Send the heads-up this weekend (Nov 28–29), one person at a time. Personal beats mass: use their name
and change a line or two. Never ask for "upvotes" (Product Hunt bans it and penalizes launches that
do); ask people to try it and leave honest feedback.

Product Hunt gives less weight to brand-new accounts that show up only on launch day, so the
heads-up gently suggests creating an account ahead of time for anyone who wants to comment.

---

## Tips

- Aim for 20–40 people who'd genuinely try it, not everyone you know.
- Space messages out over the weekend; personal replies matter more than reach.
- Keep a list of who said yes, so the launch-morning message goes only to them.
- Don't mention the Uneed launch (Dec 10) in these messages; one ask per week.
