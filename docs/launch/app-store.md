## Review notes (App Review Information)

> Sign in with "Continue with Apple" (any Apple ID works), or "Continue with email" with any address: a one-time code is emailed. No password exists. To see a full locker quickly: tap "Invite someone", copy the link, open it on another device or in Safari, fill in sizes, and the person appears in the locker. AI gift ideas: open a person → "Find the perfect gift". Account deletion: profile icon (top right) → Settings → Delete account.
